          How lessons work
        5. New items come in WaniKani's order: lowest level first, radicals, then kanji, then vocabulary.
        6. Each item has pages for its parts, meaning, reading and example sentences. Use → / enter and ← to move through them, and p or 🔊 to hear it.

          13. Reviews

          Checking WaniKani…

          How voice answers work
        13. Say the English meaning, or the Japanese reading, when the bar asks for it. You can also type (romaji turns into kana).
        14. Say "I don't know" (or わかりません) to see the answer.
        15. After a wrong answer, say "next" to count it, or "again" if it misheard you. Nothing counts as wrong until then. Press p or 🔊 to hear the reading, and m or ▶ to hear what the mic recorded: if that sounds right, it was the speech-to-text.
        16. Short readings starting with r (りょう, りゅう) are hard for speech-to-text on their own. Say them twice (りょう、りょう) or as 読みはりょう.
        17. With "let me check the results" on, nothing goes to WaniKani until you've looked over the list at the end. You can mark a miss as correct there, or untick an item to leave it due.
        18. Vowel length is forgiven (speech-to-text often hears がっこう as がっこ). A near miss, like じ for し, asks you to say it again.
        19. Sending results needs a token with the reviews:create permission (WaniKani → Settings → API Tokens). Unsent results wait here and resend.
        20. Speech-to-text runs on base-station's GPU (KBU as a backup), so voice answers need one of them on.

            Audio

            VOICEVOX setup
          1. Install VOICEVOX from voicevox.hiroshiba.jp and start it — its engine listens on localhost:50021.
          2. Serve this page from localhost so the engine accepts requests: run python3 -m http.server 8000 in the folder with this file, then open http://localhost:8000/wanikani_listening_practice.html.
          3. If Connect fails from a file:// page, open the engine's settings at 127.0.0.1:50021/setting and set CORS Policy Mode to all (or launch the engine with --cors_policy_mode all).
          4. Click Connect, pick a speaker, then Test voice.

            5. Voice quality depends on your browser/OS. Microsoft Edge has excellent natural Japanese voices; Chrome's "Google 日本語" is good too. On macOS you can add voices in System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content.
